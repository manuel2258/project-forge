1. The queue is home. Everything else is reached from it, from the title menu, or from links.
  2. One decision per place. A decision lives in the row pinned under the top bar, on the page that shows what is decided. The queue links there.
  3. The bottom navigates and replies. Strip = views, composer = messages. Never a decision.
  4. Nothing transient takes a decision's place. Toasts float and cannot be tapped; disconnect changes the bar itself.
  5. Layout never moves under a finger. Disabled keeps size and place; panels overlay.
  6. Colour means something. Amber waits on you, blue runs, green passed, yellow nears a limit, red failed.
  7. Vertical first. Phone and portrait desktop are primary; wide desktop adds columns, never reorders.
